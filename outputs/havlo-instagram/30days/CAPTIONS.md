## DAY 25 · How cross-border works
`day-25-howto-crossborder.png`

> Quick explainer: how cross-border pricing works on Havlo.
> Paste a link. We check local stores AND cross-border stores that ship to you.
> Cross-border prices include an estimated ~30% landed cost.
> The cheapest TOTAL wins. Not the cheapest sticker.

## DAY 26 · Why some deals show 0% off
`day-26-howto-zero.png`

> A small thing we won't pretend about.
> Many honest stores (pharmacies, grocers, some Shopify shops) don't surface a "compare-at" price.
> They list at retail. We show them anyway — sometimes the retail price IS the deal.
> Filter by "on sale" if you only want marked-down inventory.
